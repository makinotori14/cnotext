\section{Linear algebra}
\begin{definition}[Linear independence]\label{def:linear-independence}
Vectors $v_1,\dots,v_n$ are linearly independent if
\[
  a_1v_1+\cdots+a_nv_n=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_1=\cdots=a_n=0.
\]
\end{definition}
Definition~\ref{def:linear-independence} applies to every finite family.
